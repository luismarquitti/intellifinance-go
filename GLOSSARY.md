# IntelliFinance

Personal and household financial ledger with deterministic ingestion, Open Finance synchronization, and Human-in-the-Loop governance for AI-assisted actions.

## Language

**Tenant**:
An isolated financial boundary representing a person, household, or business that owns accounts, categories, and transactions.
_Avoid_: Workspace, Organization, Account, Household

**Account**:
A financial ledger container representing a checking account, savings reserve, cash reserve, investment portfolio, or credit card line belonging to a tenant.
_Avoid_: Wallet, Bank, Ledger, Bucket

**Liquid Account**:
An active, non-deleted checking, savings, or cash account whose balances represent immediately spendable reserves.
_Avoid_: Cash Account, Checking Only, Available Account

**Transaction**:
A dated, signed monetary movement recorded against one account, classified as an income, expense, or internal transfer leg.
_Avoid_: Entry, Journal Entry, Ledger Movement, Launch, Record

**Ledger Balance**:
The account balance computed strictly from settled transactions (`COMPLETED` or `RECONCILED`) committed to the ledger.
_Avoid_: Real Balance, Bank Balance, Current Balance

**Provider Balance Snapshot**:
A point-in-time balance observation reported by an external Open Finance provider, stored separately from the ledger balance for reconciliation.
_Avoid_: External Balance, Sync Balance, Bank Balance

**Internal Transfer**:
A cashflow-neutral movement between two accounts of the same tenant, represented by two linked transactions whose signed amounts sum to zero.
_Avoid_: Mutual Movement, Peer Transaction, Wire, Self-Transfer

**Outflow Leg**:
The negative-amount transaction leaving the source account in an internal transfer pair.
_Avoid_: Debit Leg, Source Transaction

**Inflow Leg**:
The positive-amount transaction entering the destination account in an internal transfer pair.
_Avoid_: Credit Leg, Target Transaction

**Invoice**:
A monthly or billing-cycle statement for a credit card account aggregating purchases and refunds toward a due date and status.
_Avoid_: Bill, Credit Card Statement, Monthly Statement

**Invoice Settlement**:
The payment event that discharges a credit card invoice from a liquid account without double-counting card purchases in consolidated cashflow.
_Avoid_: Bill Payment Expense, Card Payoff

**Pending Action**:
A proposed ledger mutation generated by an AI agent or automated scanner that awaits explicit human approval or rejection before execution.
_Avoid_: AI Suggestion, Proposal, Draft Mutation, Approval Task

**Canonical Source**:
An immutable, content-addressed original bank statement retained with cryptographic provenance prior to atomic ledger ingestion.
_Avoid_: Raw CSV, Statement Upload, Bank Dump

**Category**:
A tenant-scoped income or expense taxonomy node used to classify transactions for budgeting and analytics.
_Avoid_: Tag, Label, Bucket, Cost Center

**Split**:
A partial attribution of a single transaction's amount and percentage to a specific tenant member for co-budgeting.
_Avoid_: Sub-transaction, Share, Allocation
